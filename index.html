Our Mission

Encouraging Young Programmers is a free educational initiative dedicated to introducing young people to the world of programming and technology. Our mission is to make programming education accessible to everyone, regardless of their previous experience. We believe that young people can become software developers, website designers, engineers, innovators and technology leaders. Through practical lessons, programming challenges and interactive exercises, we encourage students to develop the skills needed to build the future.
